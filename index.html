Biography

Victor is a Nigeria-born student now living in the United Kingdom, whose journey is defined by creativity, determination, and a passion for both personal and professional growth.

His story began in Nigeria, where he attended Calvary International School for primary education and Preston International School for secondary education. Alongside academics, Victor nurtured his love for music, mastering piano and trumpet, earning a Muson Preliminary Grade in Piano in 2020, and performing in school choir and brass band.

Victor also explored the world of graphic design with Photoshop, Illustrator, and CorelDRAW, while developing a strong interest in computing. In the UK, he completed a BTEC Extended Certificate in Computing (2023) and a National Diploma in Computing (2024–2025), participating in NHS collaboration projects and earning a Certificate of Achievement.

Now admitted to the University of Derby for Computer Science, Victor also studies animation and motion graphics, combining technical expertise with creativity. His skillset spans problem-solving, communication, teamwork, public relations, marketing, and digital innovation.

Outside academics, Victor enjoys football, volleyball, basketball, video games, music, writing, and creative projects such as digital animations. His journey reflects a balance between creativity, technology, and personal growth, with a clear vision of becoming a Software and Network Architect.
